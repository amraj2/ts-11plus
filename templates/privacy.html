Privacy, in plain English

We don't collect anything about your child. There are no accounts, no sign-up, no adverts, no analytics and no tracking cookies.

What is stored, and where

Practice scores and Spark Stumble progress (coins, crowns, level, the bean's colour and hat, and the chosen nickname) are saved only in this browser on this device using local storage. They are never sent to a server, so we can't see them.

Nicknames are picked from a fixed list of silly words, so children can't type their real name. There is no chat and no way to talk to other players. Every other racer in Spark Stumble is a computer-controlled bot.

Clearing it

Clear this site's data in your browser settings to remove everything stored on the device.

About the questions

The questions are original practice material written for this app in the style of 11+ tests. They are not official exam papers and are not affiliated with any exam provider or publisher.
